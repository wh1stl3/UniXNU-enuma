# UFS backing: host-only capture contract and feasibility

Status: **design and synthetic host interpreter only; self-checked, not independently verified**. No probe, device entry, firmware patch, deployed image or changed gate. Base evidence: [handoff design](ufs-handoff-design.md); exact binary identity and instructions in ignored `.cache/ufs-handoff-boundary/`. No new public sources.

## Decision: capture is not yet implementable on this binary

One complete, lossless run could expose an actual live overlapping range or an observed premature release, and can characterize the last *observed* synchronous request. It cannot establish absent AP/runtime/security-domain producers, absence of uninstrumented DMA, future behavior, mapping semantics, or all allocator lifetimes. In particular a successful synchronous ReadBlocks return is not a proof of all asynchronous completion or timeout cancellation.

DarwinBoot source has usable window and EBS sites, but starts after UFSDxe initialization; a BlockIo wrapper at `15f4`/`16ac` would observe some calls, not internal allocation, reuse, private interfaces or timer-driven `4008→4210→3ec0` asynchronous requests. Wrapping gBS AllocatePages misses `7c9c` internal reuse, pool origins, caller release, and private mappings. `7dfc` and `7e2c` call the unidentified `1a958+38/+40` interface: inputs/outputs cannot responsibly be labeled PA/map/unmap until that interface and lifetime are established. No proven source hook captures all required categories from driver initialization through EBS; no safe exhaustive closed-binary interception or output path is established. **Do not request an observational boot on this plan.**

Alternative: a separately reviewed source-available UFS provider which owns descriptor, task, request data, bounce and mappings end-to-end, starts before its first allocation, and explicitly holds resources on all error/timeout paths until a contractually confirmed quiescence; wire it as the local provider instead of the opaque producer only after establishing ownership and coexistence with firmware and native handoff. This is a design direction, not an existing API, a claimed replacement, or permission to modify DMA. If provider ownership cannot be established, stop before any native reuse; it may require local control or earliest reservation including caller buffers, not a fabricated global Stop.

## Minimal events and timing, if hooks become available

All RVAs are for UFSDxe SHA256 `ffeaed467f8251b7e30a45075da84a1c563e2bac7dc4be0f79f0ed49a88b8dc9`; source association is not reproducible-build attestation. Hook requirements, **not a patch recipe**:

1. Before `e0f0` call and immediately at return `e0f4`, associate request-list allocation with allocator path `13efc→7fec→7c9c`; at reuse branch `7f2c` and fresh AllocatePages calls `7dbc/7ddc`, capture actual returned allocation extent, type and unique generation. Observe success/failure before any release, not a stale pointer snapshot. `e114/e11c` assign descriptor/task aliases; `e28c→e2bc/e2d8/e314–e328` propagate aliases, not independent allocation. Raw pointer is not automatically PA.
2. Before and after calls `7dfc` and `7e2c`, capture existing arguments, status and only contractually identified outputs; distinguish actual physical extent from IOVA. Their unmap/release and other mapping callers remain unidentified: **coverage blocker**. Identify real release/reuse sites and pool ownership before implementation; log immediately before mutation and after success. Never dereference an already released object.
3. At `15f4/16ac→3700` capture caller classification and request identity; at `d940→dabc–dae4` correlate actual data/PRDT; at `dbb0` record submit, and at the real completion path record success/failure. `dbec→c1f8–c20c→dda8` timeout is not completion. Also cover timer/async, task and non-BlockIo producers or mark coverage unknown. Caller buffer allocation/free and bounce attribution need their actual owning hooks, not a late memory-map lookup. PA/length/type must come from a proven allocation/mapping contract; absent proof mark unknown.
4. DarwinBoot `419–440,516–529`: after successful window allocation record half-open PA interval. `779–783,812–825`: record EBS attempt immediately before call and returned status immediately after; one failed attempt or retry makes the whole capture incomplete. EBS notifications may still submit. Do not add controller writes, test requests, reads of unfamiliar MMIO/pages, or release failed resources. Ordinary firmware boot behavior may itself write; this is not a zero-system-write claim.

Closed binary patching is not specified as feasible: candidate interception points above are not a verified patch/cave. No code cave, relocation-safe displaced instructions, branch range, ABI/live register/NZCV/SIMD preservation, stack alignment, TPL/reentrancy/concurrent writers, instruction/data cache maintenance or secure-boot compatibility has been proved. Without these, implementing even the listed candidate hooks would invent a patch. A future concrete diff must identify exact bytes, cave and return addresses and justify each risk; otherwise use source provider instead.

## Output, schema, and interpretation

Future capture uses a fixed-size buffer allocated before relevant allocations and before final memory-map acquisition; bounded append, explicit sequence/loss/overflow, no allocation, UFS logging, or map-key disturbance in hooks. A concurrency-safe producer scheme and cache visibility require independent proof, not assumed by this document. Existing Advanced Logger export and DarwinBoot Print→ramoops were observed historically (`README.md` stage 3/4), but neither proves safe lossless export after EBS or during these hooks. Only drain in a proved legal pre-EBS context; this cannot include EBS return. A preallocated framebuffer final summary might survive, but its preservation, capacity, final-status rendering and host retrieval are unproved. Thus **no complete output channel is currently established**; never replace missing events with zeros. No runtime logging implementation or enabled entry exists.

Host JSON schema v1 (synthetic interchange, not a firmware ABI): object `{schema:1,lost:0,overflow:false,events:[...]}`; events contiguous `seq` from zero. `window {pa,length}`; `alloc {id,category,owner,efi_type,source,pa,length}` where category is descriptor/data/bounce/task/mapping, owner loader/firmware/caller/unknown, source pages/reuse/unknown; `map|unmap|release|reuse {id}`; `submit {request,ids:[id...]}`; `complete {request,status:success|failure|timeout}`; `ebs {attempt,status}`; final `end`. Addresses and lengths are unsigned 64-bit integers; intervals are half-open. Each ID denotes a generation, never a reused pointer identity. Events must be captured before lifetime ends; known PA means independently proven PA, not pointer or IOVA. Full source coverage is deliberately not expressible as an unchecked boolean.

Run `python scripts/ufs-handoff-log.py <json>`: `RISK` (exit 0) means an observed live range overlaps the window, demanding investigation of earliest protection/local control; **not proof** of actual corruption. `UNKNOWN` (exit 2) includes incomplete, invalid, loss, failed/retried EBS, unknown origin, non-overlap without exhaustive coverage or unresolved outstanding DMA; do not adopt. Malformed ranges and active DMA release/unmap are rejected as UNKNOWN rather than treated as safety. Even a non-overlap result is not a PASS. An observed release/reuse before completion merits local control/retention investigation; invalid event ordering prevents concluding exactly what occurred without raw review. A genuine event stream requires integrity, source identity and completeness proof beyond this toy interpreter.

## Review and stop conditions

Ordinary reviewer: (1) do candidate sites actually separate PA from IOVA and cover internal reuse, caller lifetime and async/task? (2) Can a fixed buffer be exported with both EBS status and loss accounting without touching map key or adding I/O? (3) Are the synthetic interpreter's rejection and overlap cases conservative? (4) Is the source-provider alternative sufficiently bounded to investigate without assuming ownership? If either 1 or 2 remains no, stop capture preparation and investigate that specific missing hook/channel or source-provider ownership offline, not a device trial.

Before any actual high-risk implementation, deep review the **exact future diff** touching provider DMA allocation/map/unmap/timeout/release, producer synchronization, firmware instrumentation/trampolines, DarwinBoot EBS/window and cross-boundary ABI or native preallocation/takeover; include displaced bytes, ownership, lifetimes, ordering, failure retention, output and tests. No such diff exists here. One capture cannot resolve all producers, mapping contract or all future executions; separate bounded platform/ownership evidence remains necessary. Gate A stays HCI21; native TR58/TM78 empty checks and TR60 stop are unchanged, not TM stop.
